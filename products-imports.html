Products & Imports

Our products, and the products we source

Shopidemic designs its own products and sources products overseas for other businesses: finding manufacturers, getting pricing and samples, and handling the import, customs and freight that get finished goods to U.S. buyers.

Our product line

Curb Buddy®

Heavy-duty rubber ramps that let construction equipment and other vehicles drive over a street curb with ease, and let forklifts drive into shipping containers. Each ramp can hold 10,000 lbs. (Not for large dump trucks or metal tracks.)

Curb Buddy is a Shopidemic product. We designed it, protected it with a U.S. design patent and a registered trademark, and brought it to market through overseas manufacturing.

Visit CurbBuddy.com

Products we've sourced

Shopidemic sources products directly from manufacturers overseas. We have requested and received pricing on everything from electric bikes and motorcycles to solar power systems, battery storage, prefab buildings, rigging hardware and consumer electronics. Product types we have priced overseas:

  • Heavy-duty rubber curb ramps (our Curb Buddy® line)
  • Electric bicycles (e-bikes)
  • Electric dirt bikes and off-road e-motos
  • Electric motorcycles
  • Solar power systems
  • Battery energy storage
  • Portable power stations
  • Prefabricated and modular buildings
  • Transport tow chains and rigging hardware
  • Industrial lifting chains
  • Wireless earbuds
  • Personal protective equipment

Looking for something not on this list? Ask us about it.

How we source and import

  • Finding and vetting overseas manufacturers
  • Requesting and comparing quotes and pricing
  • Samples and quality checks
  • Custom tooling and molds for our own designs
  • Import and customs, freight forwarding, and U.S. delivery
  • Packaging, labeling and barcodes
  • Wholesale to dealers, distributors and resellers
  • Online selling and product listings
