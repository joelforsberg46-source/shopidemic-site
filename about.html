About Shopidemic

Shopidemic LLC is a Minnesota company founded in 2015 by Joel Forsberg. It began importing and selling products, then developed its own: the Curb Buddy® ramp, which Shopidemic designed, patented, trademarked and brought to market through overseas manufacturing.

Today Shopidemic is the home for Joel's work outside of construction: product development and imports, wholesale, business consulting, travel, and operations and logistics for small businesses and nonprofits.

Joel Forsberg, Owner

Joel has started, run and sold businesses for 30 years, beginning with a landscape construction company he founded in 1996. He was president and co-owner of a consumer product company that sold its product line to a national manufacturer in 2009, and in 2010 he started a Twin Cities landscape construction company that he built and ran for more than 15 years. Since 2001 he has worked on building, logistics and outreach projects for nonprofits in India, Rwanda, Cambodia and other countries. He holds a B.A. in Communication from Northwestern College in St. Paul (now University of Northwestern – St. Paul).
