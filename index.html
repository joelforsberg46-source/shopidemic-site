Product development, imports and practical business help

We build products, bring them to market, and help businesses get things done.

Shopidemic LLC is a Minnesota company that develops and imports products and helps small businesses and nonprofits get things done. We take products from the first sketch to overseas manufacturing and delivery to U.S. customers, our own Curb Buddy® ramp among them, and we source products and pricing overseas for other businesses. We also offer business consulting, travel planning, and operations and logistics support, in the U.S. and abroad.
